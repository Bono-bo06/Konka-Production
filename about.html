About Konka Productions

Konka Productions (Pty) Ltd is an event production and equipment hire company based in Limpopo. We provide professional production equipment and technical services for events, entertainment productions, corporate functions, and special occasions.

Our Mission and Vision

Mission: To provide reliable, professional, and affordable production equipment and services that help customers deliver successful events and productions.

Vision: To become a trusted production and equipment hire company in South Africa, recognised for quality equipment, professional service, and dependable event support.

Who We Serve

We cater to a wide variety of clients including:

  • Event organisers and wedding planners
  • DJs, musicians, and bands
  • Schools and educational institutions
  • Churches and religious organisations
  • Corporate companies and municipalities
